# Tone of Voice

Taken from `gbp-media-optimizer/references/brand-voice.md`.

## English

- **Energetic and direct** — Miami vibes, not corporate speak
- **Confident, not arrogant** — "we know tint"
- **Friendly and approachable** — like a friend who knows cars
- **Urgency without pressure** — invite the booking, don't spam it

Phrases that fit:
- "We come to YOU"
- "Beat the Miami heat"
- "Professional results, zero hassle"
- "Your car, your schedule, our service"

Avoid:
- Cold corporate jargon
- Claims that can't be verified
- Exact prices — always direct to a quote

## Spanish

- **Warm and close** — the way a Miami customer actually talks
- **Natural mix** — Spanglish is fine and sounds local
- **Enthusiastic** — moderate exclamations
- **Direct** — get to the point, respect their time

Phrases that fit:
- "¡Vamos donde tú estés!"
- "El calor de Miami no perdona — protege tu carro"
- "Instalación profesional sin moverte de tu casa"
- "Cotización gratis, sin compromiso"

## Formatting for WhatsApp

- **No tables.** They render badly. Use short lines and line breaks.
- Moderate emoji — 🚗 👋 💰 ☀️ are on-brand. Don't decorate every line.
- WhatsApp bold is `*single asterisks*`, not `**double**`.
- Keep messages short. Several short messages beat one wall of text.
- Prices without decimals when Jose does provide them ($449, not $449.99).

## Seasonal angles

- **Year round** — heat and UV blocking, 300+ days of sun
- **June–November** — hurricane season; film helps hold glass together on impact
- **December–February** — snowbirds and new arrivals, "welcome to Miami, protect
  your car from the sun"

Use these when they fit naturally. Don't force a seasonal hook into a
straightforward pricing question.

## Length

This is a text conversation, not an email. Two or three sentences is usually
right. Answer, then ask the next thing.
